## 🫧 Bubbles
Welcome to **TheClaudeSpa™**. Open `bubbles/index.html` and drag the sponge along Claude's muddy tentacles to scrub them clean. Pop bubbles for fun, and roll back in the mud to start over.

### 🟤 Mud Run
Hit **Sneak off to the mud** in the spa to play. Roll Claude through puddles to get filthy while the spa staff's rubber-gloved hands try to drag it back to the bath:

- **Move:** arrow keys / WASD, or hold and drag
- **SPLAT!** (Space or the button): dash, and fling mud off every tentacle. Splatted hands run away in disgust.
- 🟡 grabbers chase you, 🩷 lungers wind up and pounce, 🩵 soapers throw soap that washes mud off, and the 💚 BIG SCRUB sweeps a whole lane. Rubber ducks are bouncy.

When you get nabbed you go back to the spa with all the mud you collected, ready for another scrub.
